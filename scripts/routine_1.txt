- **Canonical SQL Name:** `record_tax_calculation_quote`
- **Responsibility:** Asserts tax authority certificate validity, consumes single-use nonce, enforces individual worker mTLS workload identity, validates verifier attestation key authorization, verifies asymmetric verifier digital signature over canonical attestation buffer (C-44A), executes deep in-database JSON extraction and parity cross-checks across all 17+ raw provider fields including line-item and jurisdiction breakdowns (C-44B), computes internal symmetric HMAC audit signature, and persists immutable statutory tax evidence under Model A (Provider-Authoritative Final Amounts). Explicitly locks `ApprovedMerchantCart` at Level 1 (`FOR SHARE`) before higher-level resources are acquired. Executed exclusively by authenticated tax worker instances under role `tax_verifier_role`.
- **Trust Boundary & Authorization (C-39, C-43, C-44A):**

  - Ordinary application workers (`app_orchestrator_role`), web users, and generic database roles have **ZERO** execute privilege on this routine (`SQLSTATE 42501`).
  - Executed via `SECURITY DEFINER` owned by `plinthly_security_admin` with fixed `SET search_path = extensions, pg_catalog;`.

- **Output:** Returns record `(evidence_id UUID, cart_id UUID, tax_quote_id VARCHAR(64), total_tax_cents BIGINT, status VARCHAR(16), recorded_at TIMESTAMPTZ, internal_audit_signature BYTEA)`.
